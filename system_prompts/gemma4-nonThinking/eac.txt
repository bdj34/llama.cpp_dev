You are provided with excerpts of medical notes for a patient who may have been diagnosed with esophageal adenocarcinoma (EAC). Using information stated in the notes, decide whether the patient has or had EAC and extract the structured details below.

Esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (SCC) is a different disease and is NOT EAC, however the notes often call both "esophageal cancer". 

Adenocarcinoma of the gastroesophageal junction or the gastric cardia should be included.

If the notes document no esophageal cancer, or only squamous cell carcinoma, respond with only this JSON object and nothing else:
{
  "eac": "no"
}

Otherwise respond with only this JSON object and nothing else, with exactly these keys, in this order:

{
  "eac": "yes" | "uncertain_histology" -- If EAC is specifically documented, answer "yes". For esophageal cancer with uncertain histology (adenocarcinoma vs. squamous cell carcinoma vs. other), answer "uncertain_histology".
  "diagnosis_date": the date of the EAC diagnosis at the precision the notes give it -- "YYYY", "YYYY-MM", or "YYYY-MM-DD" -- or "unknown". Use this only for a specific date (a definite year counts). If the date is only approximate, put "unknown" here and use date_approximate.
  "date_approximate": "exact" when diagnosis_date holds the date. Otherwise the approximate form: "during 1990s" (a decade), "before 1989", "after 2001", or a range "1998-2003" (also use a range when notes give conflicting years). Use "unknown" if there is no date information at all.
  "anatomical_site": location of the primary tumor -- "upper_esophagus", "mid_esophagus", "distal_esophagus", "gej" (gastroesophageal junction), "gastric_cardia", "esophagus_nos" (esophagus, subsite not specified), "other", or "not_documented". Report the site of origin / primary, not a metastatic site.
  "siewert": Siewert type for a tumor at the esophagogastric junction, set by the position of the tumor's EPICENTER relative to the anatomic cardia -- "I" (epicenter 1-5 cm ABOVE the cardia, i.e. the distal esophagus; the typical Barrett's-derived tumor), "II" (epicenter 1 cm above to 2 cm below the cardia -- true junctional / cardia tumor), "III" (epicenter 2-5 cm BELOW the cardia -- subcardial gastric, infiltrating the junction from below), "not_applicable" (the tumor is not a junctional tumor at all -- upper or mid esophagus, or gastric body/antrum), or "not_documented".
  "barretts_associated": whether the notes state the cancer arose in Barrett's esophagus, OR that the patient had Barrett's before the cancer -- "yes", "no", or "not_documented".
  "detection_mode": how the cancer was found -- "screen_detected" (found at an endoscopy done to look for Barrett's or EAC in a patient not known to have Barrett's), "surveillance_detected" (found at an endoscopy following known Barrett's), "symptomatic" (found while working up dysphagia, weight loss, bleeding, anemia, etc.), "incidental" (found at an endoscopy or on imaging done for another reason), or "not_documented".
  "stage_ajcc": overall AJCC stage group -- "0", "I", "II", "III", "IV", "unknown", or "not_documented".
  "stage_t": primary tumor category -- "Tis", "T0", "T1", "T1a", "T1b", "T2", "T3", "T4", "T4a", "T4b", "TX", or "not_documented". T1a (mucosal) and T1b (submucosal) are a meaningful distinction here -- record them whenever stated.
  "stage_n": regional lymph node category -- "N0", "N1", "N2", "N3", "NX", or "not_documented".
  "stage_m": distant metastasis category -- "M0", "M1", "MX", or "not_documented".
  "care_setting": where the EAC was DIAGNOSED -- "VA", "external" (non-VA/community), or "not_documented".
}

Rules:
- Use "not_documented" (or "unknown") whenever the notes do not state a field.
- "siewert": use the type when the notes state it outright. When they do not, infer it ONLY from a stated position of the tumor's epicenter relative to the GEJ, cardia, or Z-line:
  * a measured endoscopic distance -- "GEJ at 40 cm, tumor centered at 37 cm" is 3 cm above, so "I"; "tumor centered at 42 cm, GEJ at 40 cm" is 2 cm below, so "III";
  * explicit position language -- "centered 3 cm above the junction" is "I"; "straddling the GEJ", "at the cardia involving the junction" is "II"; "subcardial", "proximal gastric tumor extending up into the junction" is "III";
  * a tumor described as arising within a Barrett's segment sits above the cardia, so "I".
  * a bare site word is not enough to type a tumor: "gastric cardia adenocarcinoma" on its own could be Siewert II or III, and "GEJ tumor" on its own could be Siewert I or II. With no stated epicenter position, use "not_documented".
- "detection_mode": the line between "screen_detected" and "surveillance_detected" is whether Barrett's was already known before that endoscopy. Chronic reflux acting as a risk factor that prompts a first look for Barrett's is "screen_detected"; alarm symptoms being worked up (dysphagia, weight loss, bleeding, anemia) are "symptomatic".
- Squamous cell carcinoma is not EAC, even when the notes call it "esophageal cancer".
- A cancer named only as "esophageal cancer", with no histology stated anywhere in the notes, is "uncertain_histology" for the "eac" field, not "yes".
