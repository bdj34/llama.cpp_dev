You are provided with excerpts of medical notes for a patient who has BOTH a colectomy (or other colon/rectal resection) and a colorectal cancer (CRC) documented within about a year of each other. Using the notes, determine the RELATIONSHIP between the colectomy and the CRC.

Classify into exactly one of:
- "colectomy_for_crc" -- the colectomy was performed BECAUSE of a known or suspected CRC: the cancer was the indication for the operation (e.g. a biopsy-proven colon cancer, then a resection to remove it).
- "crc_found_at_colectomy" -- the colectomy was performed for a DIFFERENT reason (IBD, diverticulitis, obstruction, a polyp, etc.) and the CRC was first diagnosed on the resection specimen -- an incidental or unexpected cancer in the removed tissue.
- "unrelated" -- the colectomy and the CRC are SEPARATE events: the cancer was neither the indication for the colectomy nor found in that colectomy's specimen (e.g. a partial colectomy for IBD, and then months later a CRC found on surveillance).
- "uncertain" -- both are documented but the notes do not make the relationship clear.
- "no_crc" -- a colectomy is documented but there is NO colorectal cancer (the cancer signal was a false positive, only planned/suspected/ruled-out, or a non-colorectal cancer).
- "no_colectomy" -- a colorectal cancer is documented but NO colon or rectal resection was actually performed (only planned, discussed, or done elsewhere without detail).
- "neither" -- the notes document neither a colectomy nor a colorectal cancer.

Respond with ONLY this JSON object and nothing else, with exactly these keys, in this order:
{
  "relationship": one of "colectomy_for_crc", "crc_found_at_colectomy", "unrelated", "uncertain", "no_crc", "no_colectomy", or "neither".
  "confidence": "low" | "medium" | "high" | "certain" -- your confidence in the relationship.
}

Rules:
- Anchor on two things: the documented INDICATION for the colectomy, and WHERE the CRC was first diagnosed (before the operation, on the specimen, or at a separate time/procedure).
- Timing coincidence is not decisive. A CRC found on the resection specimen is "crc_found_at_colectomy" even though the dates coincide; a CRC found weeks or months after a colectomy done for another reason is "unrelated".
- Do NOT assume the colectomy was cancer-directed just because a cancer exists -- check the stated reason for the operation.
- Use "uncertain" only when BOTH are genuinely documented but the relationship is not stated. When one or both are not actually documented, use "no_crc" (colectomy only), "no_colectomy" (CRC only), or "neither".
- Think briefly, a few sentences of reasoning at most. Weigh only the decisive evidence, then stop thinking and give the answer.
